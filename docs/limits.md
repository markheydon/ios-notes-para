# What Notes will not do

The system in this guide is shaped around Notes on iPhone and iPad. Knowing the app’s real limits saves you from fighting the software. The guide assumes **iOS 27** or **iPadOS 27**.

## It is not a task manager

Checklists do not have contexts, defer dates, or a weekly review of next actions. If you build GTD inside Notes, you will re-create Reminders badly. Put actions in Reminders. Put material here. The sibling [Reminders GTD](https://markheydon.me.uk/ios-reminders-gtd/) guide is the other half.

## There is no graph

Notes can link to another note. It will not show backlinks, a map of content, or unlinked mentions. If you need that, you wanted Obsidian. Search, folders, and a few deliberate links are the retrieval model here.

## Smart Folders are not folders

**Make into Smart Folder** on an ordinary folder moves its notes out and tags them. That is why **Projects**, **Areas**, **Resources**, and **Archives** must stay ordinary. Smart Folders are saved searches. They cannot be the place a note lives.

## Tags and locks do not mix

You cannot lock a tagged note, and you cannot tag a locked note. Password notes are a separate pile. Keep secrets out of PARA tags. A locked note can still sit in an ordinary folder.

## IMAP is the wrong account

Notes attached to an email account miss features this system uses: reliable tags, Smart Folders, and the folder behaviour you expect on iCloud. Glance at a work IMAP mailbox if you must. Build PARA in iCloud.

On My iPhone notes will not show up on the iPad. Do not hide a project there.

## The web app is a viewer

iCloud.com can show notes. Capture, Quick Note, the document scanner, widgets, and the way folders behave on a phone are the system. Set it up and run the review on your iPhone or iPad.

The **Action button** and some **Lock Screen** capture options are iPhone-only. On iPad, use Quick Note, the share sheet, Siri, Control Centre, or the app icon.

The Mac app can edit the same iCloud folders. The instructions here follow the iPhone and iPad controls on purpose, because folder nesting, pinning, and Smart Folder conversion are easy to mis-tap when a guide mixes two interfaces.

## Search is spelling, not meaning

Notes search is not a vector database. It will not find “the plumber thing” if you titled the note “Boiler quiet again” and never wrote “plumber”. Put the words you will remember in the title or the first lines.

## Collaboration is one note at a time

Sharing a folder or a note is fine. Sharing your entire **Projects** tree mixes other people’s inbox with your review. Share the smallest unit that needs two editors.

## Folders do not count the way you wish

A folder that only contains subfolders can show **0** notes. That does not mean it is empty. Open it. This is easy to misread during the review.

## Intelligence can sound sure

A generated summary can drop the one number you cared about. Read it. The two-line summary you write in [find it and use it](retrieve-and-express.md) is still the durable distill.

## A new folder will not rescue a skipped review

A fifth top-level bucket feels like progress and leaves Inbox just as full. If the system feels heavy, archive a finished project before you add a Smart Folder, and do a short review: **Notes** folder, then **Projects**.
