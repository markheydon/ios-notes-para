# A worked week

A fictional week so the folders have somewhere to sit. The person here has a job, a house, and a talk they agreed to give. Names are invented.

## Monday: capture without filing

On the train, a podcast mentions a billing trick. Share sheet, Notes, title left as the episode name. It lands in the built-in **Notes** folder.

At lunch, the boiler makes a new noise. Siri: “Make a note: boiler banging on hot water, happened at noon.” Same inbox.

In the afternoon a colleague mails a PDF about the talk. Share to Notes. Still inbox. No folders yet. The point of Monday is not virtue. The point is that none of this stayed in Mail or in their head.

## Monday evening: a ten-minute empty

Three notes in **Notes**:

- Podcast billing trick → might be useful, no commitment → **Resources**. Title becomes “Billing: usage credits”.
- Boiler banging → there is a finish line (quiet boiler) and they are committed → **Projects**. Title becomes “Boiler quiet again”. They add a checklist row “Find last service PDF”. The actual call to the engineer goes in Reminders, tagged `home`.
- Talk PDF → supports the existing project note “Conference talk delivered” → moved into that note as an attachment, inbox copy deleted.

Inbox is empty. They did not open Archives.

## Tuesday: use a project note

They have thirty minutes before a meeting. They open **Projects → Conference talk delivered**, not search. Yesterday’s PDF is there. They add a heading “Running order” and three bullets. That is distill plus express. Reminders still holds “Draft slides for the opening three minutes” as an `anywhere` action.

They do not create a Talks subfolder. One note is still enough.

## Wednesday: an area, not a project

The dentist sends a reminder about a check-up. That is a next action (Reminders, `call`). The new address and NHS number belong in **Areas**, in the note “Personal health”. They paste them under a heading “Dentists”. No finish line on “being a person who has a dentist”.

## Thursday: the project grows a folder

The boiler engineer wants the last three invoices and a photo of the model plate. “Boiler quiet again” now has several pages. They create a subfolder **Projects → Boiler quiet again**, drop the original note in, add “Model plate photo” and “Invoices 2024–2026”. The Reminders project stays one outcome; Notes now has a small pile.

They pin “Boiler quiet again” while the engineer is in flight, and will unpin it when the job is done.

## Friday: weekly review

**Notes** folder: two new captures. A recipe screenshot goes to **Resources**. A “maybe start a newsletter” dump stays once; they will not promote it without a real commitment. Second week it will go to **Resources** or the bin.

**Projects:** “Conference talk delivered” still true. “Boiler quiet again” still true. An old note “Refresh the team wiki” has had no action in a month - they were never committed. It moves to **Resources**.

**Areas:** “Personal health” picked up the dentist. “Money” is unchanged. They do not rewrite Money.

They open “Conference talk delivered” and actually write the opening paragraph into the note. Review ends with express, not with a new folder.

## The following Monday: archive

The boiler is quiet. They move **Projects → Boiler quiet again** to **Archives**. The certificates that must remain findable get copied (or linked) into **Areas → Home**. Reminders: complete the outcome.

Search would still find “Model plate photo”. It no longer clutters the active project list.

## What this week did not do

- No Smart Folders.
- No `#boiler` tag.
- No second PARA tree for Work.
- No highlighting on the podcast note, which they have not reopened.

That is a successful week.
