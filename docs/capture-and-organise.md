# Capture and organise

Capture is supposed to be cheap. Organise is supposed to be delayed. Mixing the two is how people stop writing notes altogether.

Tiago Forte’s CODE loop is Capture, Organise, Distill, Express. This page is the first two. Distill and Express are [find it and use it](retrieve-and-express.md).

## Capture

When something shows up - a quote, a screenshot, a voice dump, a link, a photo of a whiteboard - put it in Notes without picking a PARA folder.

Good capture paths:

- The compose button inside Notes
- The share sheet from Safari, Mail, Photos, or Files
- Siri: “Make a note…”
- Quick Note from Control Centre, the Action button, or (on iPad) the corner swipe
- A scan: compose, then the camera / document scanner, when the paper has to live with the project

The title can be lazy. A first line of “plumber quote” is enough. You can tidy titles when you file.

Do not:

- Create a new subfolder in the moment
- Apply `#work` or `#personal` unless it is genuinely zero extra thought
- Turn the note into a task list of next actions (that is Reminders)

If the capture *is* a next action and there is no material to keep, skip Notes and send it to Reminders Inbox instead. A second brain that duplicates every errand is a slower GTD.

## What “in Inbox” means

The built-in **Notes** folder is Inbox. A note still in that folder has not been given a job. It might be gold. It might be nothing. You do not know yet, and that is fine for a few days.

It is not fine for a few months. Inbox is a queue, not a library.

## Organise: empty Inbox

Once a day if the pile is small, and at least in the [weekly review](weekly-review.md), walk the **Notes** folder from the top.

For each note, ask in this order:

1. **Trash?** Delete it. Recently Deleted is a safety net.
2. **Is it a next action with no reference value?** Move the action to Reminders, then delete the note.
3. **Finish line, and you are committed?** Move to **Projects**. Rename the title to the outcome if you can.
4. **Ongoing standard?** Move to **Areas**.
5. **Useful later, no commitment?** Move to **Resources**.
6. **Already finished or on ice?** Move to **Archives**.
7. **Still cannot decide?** Leave it for the next review once. The second time you skip it, it is either a resource or trash.

To move a note: in the list, swipe left or touch and hold, tap **Move**, choose the folder. On iPad or in landscape you may see Move in the toolbar instead.

If several notes belong to the same active project, that is the moment to create **one** subfolder under **Projects** and drop them in. Do not create the subfolder first and hope notes appear.

## Titles worth searching for

When you file, spend five seconds on the first line.

Poor: “Meeting”, “Scan”, “Ideas”.
Better: “Board pack 12 May”, “Boiler service 2026”, “Talk: notes on pacing”.

Search in Notes matches titles and body. A specific title is the cheapest retrieval upgrade you will ever make.

## Attachments stay with the note

Photos, PDFs, and scans should ride inside the project or resource note they belong to. Mirroring PARA in the Files app is optional and lives in [advanced](advanced.md). For most people, “the scan is in the project note” is enough.

## What you are not doing yet

You are not highlighting, summarising, or writing from the note during organise. File it. If the thought is “I should turn this into a post”, that *project* is the finish line; the captured article is supporting material. Put the project note in **Projects** and, if you still want the original, leave it in **Resources** or paste a link.

Organise ends when **Notes** is empty, or empty except for captures from the last day that you have not had a chance to look at. Then close the folder list and go do some work.
