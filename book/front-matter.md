# Notes PARA

An opinionated PARA / second-brain setup for the iPhone and iPad **Notes** app on **iOS 27** and **iPadOS 27**.

Tiago Forte’s PARA method and Building a Second Brain are the foundation. This guide is independent work: it is not an Apple product and it is not endorsed by Tiago Forte or Forte Labs.

The canonical web edition is at [markheydon.me.uk/ios-notes-para](https://markheydon.me.uk/ios-notes-para/).

![Capture lands in Notes, then files into Projects, Areas, Resources, or Archives](images/system-map.svg)

If you only do three things: create the four folders in the [setup](setup.md) guide, treat the built-in **Notes** folder as Inbox, and empty it weekly.

Next actions belong in Reminders. The sibling guide is [Reminders GTD](https://markheydon.me.uk/ios-reminders-gtd/).

## What you need

- An iPhone on iOS 27 or an iPad on iPadOS 27, signed into iCloud with Notes turned on. Use the **iCloud** account in Notes, not an IMAP mailbox. Apple Intelligence extras, such as summarising a long note, need a supported device (for example iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with Apple Intelligence). The folders themselves do not.
- About an hour the first time, then a short weekly review
- No extra apps
